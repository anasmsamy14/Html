Did You Know?

In this webpage we are going to tell you some interesting facts about coding and programming.

Fact 1: The First Programmer

Did you know that he First Programmer: Ada Lovelace wrote the world's first algorithm in 1843 for Charles Babbage's mechanical computer, the Analytical Engine. She is often considered the first computer programmer and her work laid the foundation for modern computing.

Fact 2: The First Computer Bug

Did you know that the term "computer bug" originated from an actual bug? In 1947, a moth was found trapped in a relay of the Harvard Mark II computer, causing it to malfunction. The engineers documented the incident as the "first actual case of bug being found," and the term stuck.

Fact 3: The Appollo 11 Guidance Computer

Did you know that the Apollo 11 Guidance Computer (AGC) used a programming language called AGC assembly language? It was a low-level language specifically designed for the AGC, and it played a crucial role in the success of the Apollo 11 mission, allowing astronauts to land on the moon in 1969.

Fact 4: Coding Languages

Did you know that there are over 700 programming languages in existence today? Some of the most popular ones include Python, JavaScript, Java, C++, and Ruby. Each language has its own unique features and is suited for different types of applications.

Fact 5: JavaScript quick creation

Did you know that JavaScript was created in just 10 days? Brendan Eich developed JavaScript in May 1995 while working at Netscape Communications Corporation. It was initially called Mocha, then renamed to LiveScript, and finally to JavaScript. Despite its rapid development, JavaScript has become one of the most widely used programming languages in the world.
