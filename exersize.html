💪 Why Your Body needs exercise

Your body is like a supercar — the more you use it, the stronger it gets! Kids who move for at least 60 minutes a day are healthier, happier, and have more energy to play and learn

Dancing,football, swimming, or even just running around with friends can help your heart, muscles, and bones grow strong. So, get moving and have fun!
