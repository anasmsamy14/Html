Thunder kid

the most powerful kid in the world

Super Powers

Thunder Kid has the ability to control electricity and generate powerful lightning bolts. He can also fly at incredible speeds and has superhuman strength.

Thunder Kid is also a skilled martial artist and has a keen intellect, allowing him to outsmart his enemies.

Weaknesses

Despite his incredible powers, Thunder Kid is vulnerable to water, which can short-circuit his electrical abilities. He also has a tendency to be overconfident, which can lead him into dangerous situations.

Thunder Kids powers are also limited by his age and experience, and he may struggle against more experienced villains.

Overall, Thunder Kid is a formidable hero with incredible powers, but he must be careful to avoid his weaknesses and continue to grow and develop his abilities.
